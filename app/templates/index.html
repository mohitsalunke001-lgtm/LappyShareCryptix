Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about peer-to-peer campus hardware lending.

Who is eligible to participate in LappyShare?

Any actively enrolled university or college student with a verified college email address. Students can participate as borrowers when they need computing power for coursework, and as lenders whenever their laptop is idle.

Are there any rental charges or hidden fees?

Zero monetary cost. LappyShare is 100% voluntary and academic-focused. LappyCoins are non-monetary merit tokens used purely for campus reputation and community recognition.

How is hardware safety and custody guaranteed?

Lenders and borrowers conduct a mandatory pre-handover hardware inspection (screen condition, ports, keyboard, charger). Handover is completed via encrypted QR code validation, and all transactions are tied to verified student IDs overseen by campus administration.

What happens if a device is returned late or damaged?

Any damage or overdue return is logged immediately during the return inspection. Accounts with overdue flags or unresolved disputes are suspended and referred to the institutional academic lab coordinator.
