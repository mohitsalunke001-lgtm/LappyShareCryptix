About LappyShare

Student-to-student solidarity through shared technology.

Our Mission

LappyShare was founded with a clear, singular vision: no engineering or college student should ever miss a practical exam, fall behind on an AI/ML lab, or abandon a technical project simply because they lack personal laptop access.

On every college campus, dozens of laptops sit unused in student hostels and dorm rooms while fellow classmates struggle to book computer center time. LappyShare creates a secure, trusted campus bridge between students who have surplus computing hardware and those in need.

Core Values

Academic Equality

Empowering every student with equal access to computational labs regardless of personal economic background.

100% Non-Monetary

Borrowing is strictly free. Community contribution is rewarded through LappyCoins recognition rather than cash.

Mutual Accountability

Clear condition logs, campus-backed verification, and administrative audit trails ensure respect and device safety.

Privacy & Trust

Personal contact numbers are kept private until an academic reservation is approved by both student parties.
