How LappyShare Works

A step-by-step walkthrough of voluntary campus laptop sharing for both borrowers and lenders.

For Students Who Need a Laptop (Borrowers)

1. Browse Available Hardware

Filter by CPU, RAM (8GB, 16GB), operating system, or desired dates. View detailed specs suitable for AI/ML, coding, or lab work.

2. Submit Borrowing Request

Select pickup and return dates, specify your academic purpose (e.g. practical exam, semester project), and add a note for the lender.

3. Meet for Campus Handover

Once accepted, coordinate a safe pickup location on campus (e.g. college library or department lobby). Verify the device condition together.

4. Return on Time & Earn Reputation

Return the laptop in good shape on or before the due date. Punctual returns earn +2 LappyCoins.

For Students Who Share Laptops (Lenders)

1. List Your Spare Device

Provide model details, RAM, storage, OS, and any specific guidelines (e.g. handle with sleeve). Receive +10 LappyCoins on listing.

2. Review Incoming Requests

See who is requesting, their department, semester, and academic purpose. Accept or decline requests based on your schedule.

3. Complete Handover Inspection

Hand over the laptop and charger in person. Record initial physical condition or use our single-use QR verification.

4. Inspect Return & Earn Rewards

Check the laptop upon return. Mark the reservation completed to receive +5 LappyCoins for contributing to community access.

Academic Use Cases Supported on LappyShare

AI & Machine Learning

Laptops with dedicated NVIDIA GPUs or high memory (16GB+) for training neural nets and running PyTorch / TensorFlow scripts.

Coding & Web Labs

Devices with modern multi-core CPUs for IDEs like VS Code, Eclipse, Android Studio, and Node.js / React full-stack frameworks.

Embedded Systems

Windows and Linux configurations for Keil uVision, Arduino IDE, MATLAB, Proteus simulations, and STM32 microcontroller tools.

Hackathons & Viva

Temporary 24-48 hour laptop loans for intense coding sprints, technical symposia, and final semester project demonstrations.
